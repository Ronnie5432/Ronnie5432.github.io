Ronnie Guidry – (555) 555-0142

Hello, my name is Ronnie Guidry and this is a website regarding my new found interest in 3D Printing. I’ve only just started, but I think I have a lot of ideas that could turn into some nice products for those interested. This website will contain 2 options when it comes to prints. A premade group with items consisting of figurines and other miscellaneous things, and a personalized order where the customer can decide what they want created within certain parameters.

My background is I was a Airforce Avionics Technician for 4 years then I went into the role of a Aerospace CNC Machinist. Currently in the process of switching roles into a more quality perspective. I also have a wife and 5 children so I’ve gotten used to juggling a lot at once. Some interest and hobbies I have are my newfound 3D printing interest and I enjoy video games, reading, and spending time with my family.
